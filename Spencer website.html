About the evidence flags. "Trial / RCT" marks recommendations anchored by randomized trials. "Guideline" marks society/consensus recommendations that may rest on observational or mixed data. "Expert / limited" marks practices driven largely by expert opinion, physiologic rationale, or low-quality evidence — common but not strongly proven. "Practice check" highlights recommendations that are frequently given but that current evidence does not support, or actively argues against.

About the citation links. Every source is clickable. A ↗ opens the source document or trial directly (publishers don't expose stable links to a specific section, so these land on the document itself). A ⌕ is a one-click lookup that searches for that exact guideline or trial — used where a verified direct link wasn't available. Always confirm against the current version of the relevant guideline.

Guideline currency (reviewed June 2026). Citations were checked for major revisions — for example, the 2025 ACC/AHA Acute Coronary Syndromes guideline (which replaced the older STEMI/NSTEMI documents) and the 2025 ACC/AHA Hypertension guideline are reflected. Note that some bodies update on their own cycles: the ADA Standards of Care, GINA (asthma), and GOLD (COPD) are revised annually — always check the current year's edition; and the KDIGO AKI guideline remains the 2012 version, with a 2026 update in public review. This tool cannot guarantee every citation is the very latest version, so confirm against the live guideline before acting on any recommendation.
